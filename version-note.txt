0.35.68: Replace the stacked collision pipeline with one capsule move-and-slide controller. Remove the full-body hard-stop, R97 collision supplement, R97 renderer/rig wrapper, triangle/body movement compatibility layer, and per-render final-contact pass. Movement now uses a nearby-collider broad phase, continuous sweep, up to six slide iterations, multi-contact normal projection for corners, and a small skin separation. Upper-body pose code no longer becomes locomotion authority. This removes the old per-frame microstep, triangle, and limb-contact workloads that could dominate frame time while moving or rendering.
