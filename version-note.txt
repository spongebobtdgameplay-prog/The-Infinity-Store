0.35.68: Remove the full-body hard-stop, R97 collision supplement, and renderer/rig wrappers. Movement now has one capsule controller with a nearby-collider broad phase, continuous sweep, up to six move-and-slide iterations, multi-contact normal projection for corners, skin separation, and no upper-body root blocking. This also removes the old per-frame microstep collision workload that could dominate frame time while moving.
