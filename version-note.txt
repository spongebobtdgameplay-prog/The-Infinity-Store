0.35.70: Replace coarse one-box-per-mesh furniture collision with engine-owned 4x4 occupied-cell compound collision, using actual triangle/cell overlap and oriented local boxes for tighter couch, shelf, sink, oven and retail-object shapes. Replace the old exact-geometry UUID InstancedMesh pass with Three.js BatchedMesh groups so different static geometries sharing a material can collapse into far fewer draw submissions. Reduce the live store window to one aisle behind and two ahead, boot three finished aisles, give gameplay generation a larger real idle-time requirement, shorten hidden far rendering, and add slow hysteresis-based adaptive render resolution only under sustained frame pressure. No Renderer.render or movement wrappers are added; engine core owns collision/batching and game.js remains visibility authority.
